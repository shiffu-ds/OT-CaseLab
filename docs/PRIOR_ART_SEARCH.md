## Positioning and next search actions

The proposed focus is linking bilingual occupational interviewing, learner-authored image observations and traceable functional-goal reasoning. Its usefulness, fairness, comparative benefit and curriculum fit remain hypotheses. Do not use “Pakistan-first”, “world-first”, “better than Simucase” or a market-size claim.

Before a research novelty statement: ask institutional librarians to search HEC repositories and local theses directly; expand Urdu and Roman Urdu terms; search OT journals and trial registries; document eligibility criteria and screened records; consult educators about existing teaching workflows. Record access failures and unmatched features as unknown. Do not reproduce proprietary case banks or rubric scales.
